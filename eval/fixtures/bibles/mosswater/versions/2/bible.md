# Mosswater Stilts (v2)

## Mood
A village of damp spruce timber standing on red mangrove legs over still water. It has weathered and leans a little, but it's quiet and easy to read: plank walls, small lit windows and one lantern at each door.

## Silhouette
Every building stands on **mangrove log stilts** 3 blocks above the water or mud. Bodies are narrow: 1–2 storeys of 4 blocks. Each building has a **low roof** at half pitch, with thin slab eaves that overhang by 1. The walls must show clearly under the eaves. Allow **one** crooked gesture per building: a ridge offset by one block, *or* an upper storey jettied by 1. Never use both on the same building.

## Roles
- **wall (spruce planks):** the whole skin. Keep it calm and continuous.
- **wall_alt (stripped spruce log):** one weather-side wall panel at most. It's a single flat area, never speckles.
- **trim (dark oak log):** only at the corner posts and the jetty beam. Don't use it anywhere else.
- **frame (mangrove log):** the stilts and boardwalk posts, below the floor line only. It also sets the doors, fences and trapdoors to mangrove.
- **roof (dark oak planks):** slabs and stairs only.
- **foundation (mossy cobblestone):** one-block pile caps where the stilts meet the mud.
- **accent / drape / creeper (moss block, moss carpet, vine):** at the waterline only, on the pile caps and lowest stilt blocks. Never put them on roofs or upper walls.
- **floor:** spruce planks. **path:** coarse dirt on the shore, spruce slab boardwalks over the water.
- **glass:** brown stained panes. **light:** lantern.

## Roof & windows
Use half-pitch gables with a slab eave line, an upside-down stair fascia and nothing on top. Set a window every 3 blocks, with at least **2 per facade, visible from the front**. Each window is a single pane at eye level with a spruce trapdoor shutter on each side. Keep windows on a steady line. The crookedness belongs to the roof, not the windows.

## Components
- **window:** a pane with two trapdoor shutters and a slab sill.
- **door_surround:** a mangrove door under a dark oak log lintel, with a slab step down to the boardwalk.
- **lantern_post:** a mangrove fence post with an arm and a hanging lantern beside the door.
- **roof_trim:** an upside-down dark oak stair fascia under slab eaves.
- **chimney:** mossy cobble, one block wide, rising straight 2 blocks above the ridge and capped with a campfire.
- **stilt_pile:** a mangrove log on a mossy cobble cap, with moss carpet on the cap.
- **boardwalk:** a 2-wide spruce slab walk on mangrove posts, with mangrove fence rails on one side.
- **net_loft:** at most one per building: a fence-and-barrel shelf under the gable end.

## Tiers
**Humble** buildings (huts, sheds, houses) use spruce walls, dark oak roofs, mangrove stilts and a single door lantern. **Important** buildings (the fish hall, the harbour master's house) add dark oak corner posts, mossy cobble caps with moss at the waterline, fence railings and a second lantern. They are bigger and taller, not busier.

## Do
- Show the three hero motifs on every building: stilts, shuttered windows, door lantern.
- Keep the accent share under 6% of any facade.
- Let the walls read: low roofs, thin eaves.

## Don't
- Don't put moss on roofs, or scatter logs and accents across walls.
- Don't use deep or steep roofs, or blank facades.
- Don't let buildings touch the ground or water.
- Don't stack several crooked tricks on one building.
