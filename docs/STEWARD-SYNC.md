# Architect ↔ Steward sync

The shared view of what Architect ships, what Steward has adopted, and the agreed order. Contracts and as-builts stay in
`docs/CONTRACT.md`; Steward's design stays in steward-mc `docs/COMPOSITION.md`; the asks either way in steward-mc
`docs/ARCHITECT-ASKS.md`.

- **"Architect ships"** is generated: `tools/release-version.mjs` writes a row of `docs/RELEASES.json` at every release (mod, API,
  kit, catalogue, the feature names added over the last release, the Steward RC state, the package state) and rewrites the block
  below. After a hand edit of `docs/RELEASES.json` (an RC passed, `tools/pub-verify.mjs` ran) run
  `node tools/release-version.mjs --sync`. `--check` fails when the block and the file disagree.
- **"Steward's side"** is kept by hand by the coordinator after each Steward pin or sync.

## Architect ships

The feature names are those `ArchitectApi.features()` can advertise (ApiRules); a sidecar-backed name appears only when the helper
has it. "Package ok" means `tools/pub-verify.mjs` found the jar, the sources jar, the pom and the module with matching checksums.
Rows before v0.60.0 were back-filled from the git tags on 2026-11-13 (slice TJ): their Steward state is in ARCHITECT-ASKS.

  route names its graph edge in its IR ext, `architect_mc:route: {edge: "route_d_<a>__<b>"}` (IR only: a road's batch item ext still
  carries only `architect_mc:street`). `Regions.layout` then lists each road piece of a route drawn whole (one with a stair or
  graded section) once, inside its `route_d_<a>__<b>` shape and not also alone; folds in only that edge's own pieces, a long road's
  groups (`<edge>_<n>_<k>`) included; and draws a route whose edge id equals another route's road piece id (districts `b` and `b_1`), which
  was missing. A route laid as roads only keeps its pieces listed alone. A region planned before keeps v0.83.0's shapes (pieces listed
  twice). `architect_mc:land`'s route parts follow the same edges; for a region planned before, a recomputed land record (a GR
  revision) reads a long road's group `route_d_a__b_3_1` as `d_a__b`'s, where it made a bogus route part `d_a__b_3`. CONTRACT "Slice RTE".
### New for Steward (NEXT, slice RTE2)
- `architect_mc:land` with districts whose ids share a prefix (`b` and `b_1`): each `district` part now holds only its own parts, lots,
  roads and following street (by the longest district id, and a street piece by the edge it names, `route_st_<id>`); district `b` no
  longer absorbs `b_1`'s. A join road's long groups (`route_join_<n>_<k>`) are in the `join` part (were `extra`), and the join's
  `district` is the longest id its start names (was the first listed: `b` for `b_1`'s join). A grown part's `district` names one district
  (was `b,b_1`). No API or record change, no new feature: plans without such ids are byte-identical. A region planned before reads the same
  way when its land is recomputed (a GR revision); untagged street pieces go to the longest district with a street edge.
- A districts route laid from NEXT on never takes a piece id that is another route's id or begins one: `route_d_a__b`'s pieces skip
  `route_d_a__b_1` when edge `route_d_a__b_1` exists (its numbers then start at 2). So b's pieces no longer take b_1's ids (the
  RTE limit where one hid the other's route shape in `Regions.layout`), and a long first section no longer refuses the plan ("road id used twice"). Piece numbers may have gaps; order by them, don't count on them. CONTRACT "Slice RTE2".

### New for Steward (NEXT, slice T4R-D; Steward's T4R-D review A2, A3, A5, A7, Q8)

- **Semantic narrowing (the one non-additive change):** on a removal begun by this build, `RemoveResult.refund` no longer lists the
  player's own blocks found in the box. They are owed (`RemoveResult.owedParts`, reason `player_blocks`) to their placer, else the site's
  recipient, else the requester, else `architect:unclaimed`; never dropped, never credited to a pile automatically (only an explicit
  `creditOwedToStock`). A removal a pre-T4R build left in flight still reports v0.89.0's refund.
- `RemoveResult.owedParts` (`OwedPart(key, recipient, reason, items)`), immutable; `Sites.removalResult(kind, id)` (kinds `site`, `road`,
  `group`, `stage` (`<group>/<stage>`), `region`, alias `revision` (`<region>#<rid>`); at most 1024 per world, oldest out).
- `ArchitectRefused.busy()` = `Busy(kind, blockingId, blockingKind, blockingOwner)`, kinds `REMOVAL_IN_PROGRESS`, `WAITING_BEHIND`,
  `PLAYER_BLOCKS_RETURNING`, `HELD_DECIDED`; `detail()` = `<kind_lower>:<blockingId>` or `held(decided)`. `Sites.remove` now fails with it
  (it answered a `RemoveResult` with the "Not yet" text in `blockers`). (Fix round P2-2) Every route does: a site that is a pending member
  of a live removal fails `HELD_DECIDED` (it answered "No site"); a road's or cell site's removal fails typed, from the call or its future
  (a road answered `RemoveResult(false)` with the text); a held single removal after its commit fails `HELD_DECIDED` with the removal's base.
- (Narrowing round) `Busy.Kind.OPERATION_IN_PROGRESS` (appended): another operation that is not a removal (a crate settlement, a take,
  a delivery, a holder's contents being taken out) holds one of the call's cells. `blockingId` is its txn id, `blockingKind` its txn kind,
  `blockingOwner` its owner or recipient; token `operation_in_progress:<txn id>`. No `REMOVAL_FINISHED` follows: retry after a few seconds.
  It replaces the `REMOVAL_IN_PROGRESS` with a made-up `txn:<key>` id such a refusal gave. A `Busy.Kind` a caller does not know now means
  "retry after a short delay", never "wait for an event".
  (Last round) A road's or region target's own removal retry is refused this way too while its own holder cargo (`removal.cargo`) is open:
  `OPERATION_IN_PROGRESS` first, then `PLAYER_BLOCKS_RETURNING` while its blocks are given back. Both are retries by time.
- (Fix round) Auto-collect also runs after a group's, stage's, region's or revision's removal and after a road's extraction. A player's
  chest in a road or region-target cell: its stacks are taken out first, plain stacks credited into the target's pile, data stacks owed
  (reason `cargo`); a holder without slots that holds items refuses the removal ("take them out").
- `RemovalStopCause.WAITING_BEHIND` (appended); `SiteGroup.removalWait()`, `RegionView.removalWait()` (`RemovalWait(cause, blocking,
  sinceTick)`), null when not waiting. `RevisionView.removalWait()` exists and is null (a revision undo's wait shows on its region's view).
- REMOVAL_FINISHED: a standalone cascade fires one event per member, each from its own snapshot; group, region and revision one aggregate.
- Auto-collect: the player's blocks go into the online recipient's inventory after the removal retires ("Returned N of your blocks from
  <site>."), what does not fit stays owed; at the next join "Architect owes you N items (from <sites>): /architect collect." Nothing at
  server start; `architect:unclaimed` never auto-collected (`/architect owed nearby` lists it).
- Dev (A3): `dev.pt.uses`/`dev.pt.uuid` give the fake placer's UUID; `dev.stockpile.owed {recipient}`; `dev.rv.undo` returns `refund`,
  `owed`, `owedParts`; `dev.removal.owners`.
